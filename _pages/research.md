## Research experience

**Honours Research Internship on Optimisation** · Donders Institute, Nijmegen · September 2025 – June 2026

Explored computational approaches to optimisation and alternative architectures for challenging combinatorial problems. Investigated unconventional computing approaches to large-scale combinatorial optimisation, including oscillatory Ising machine architectures for NP-hard problems.

## Selected projects

### Explainable AI for Drug Response Prediction

Built end-to-end preprocessing and machine learning pipelines for high-dimensional biomedical data, including feature engineering, model training, and evaluation. Compared SHAP and Integrated Gradients using a deletion-based faithfulness metric: scores of 0.072 and 0.0858, respectively, against a 0.031 random baseline. The work focused on implementation and explainability evaluation, without claiming strong predictive accuracy.

### GAN Fine-Tuning and Evaluation for Image Synthesis

Deployed a modular Deep Convolutional GAN in PyTorch for image synthesis. Investigated learning-rate scheduling, architectural ablations, and hyperparameter optimisation to improve training stability.

### Knowledge Graph Pipeline for Research Metadata

Designed a Neo4j knowledge graph for scholarly research metadata. Built a Python batch ingestion pipeline for the OpenAlex REST API with Pydantic validation of duplicate identifiers, missing references, and invalid records.

### Monte Carlo Policy Evaluation in a Markov Decision Process

Implemented Monte Carlo policy evaluation and control for a custom Markov Decision Process using greedy and ε-soft exploration. Analysed policy convergence and exploitation trade-offs through training diagnostics and simulation-based evaluation.
