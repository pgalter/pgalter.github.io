Hello, I'm Paula. I study Applied Mathematics and Statistics on the PhD track at [École Polytechnique](https://www.polytechnique.edu/en), after graduating with honours in Artificial Intelligence from Radboud University.

My interests span machine learning, optimisation, mathematical foundations of AI, and the evaluation of intelligent systems. At the Donders Institute, I investigated unconventional approaches to combinatorial optimisation, including oscillatory Ising machine architectures. My projects also explore explainable AI, generative models, knowledge graphs, and reinforcement learning.

I enjoy connecting theoretical ideas with practical systems. See my [research and projects]({{ '/research/' | relative_url }}) or read my [full CV]({{ '/cv/' | relative_url }}).
