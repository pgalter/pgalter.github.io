Paula Galter

École Polytechnique · AI, ML & Mathematics

Hello, I’m Paula. I study Applied Mathematics and Statistics on the PhD track at École Polytechnique, after graduating with honours in Artificial Intelligence from Radboud University.

My interests span machine learning, optimisation, mathematical foundations of AI, and the evaluation of intelligent systems. At the Donders Institute, I investigated unconventional approaches to combinatorial optimisation, including oscillatory Ising machine architectures. My projects also explore explainable AI, generative models, knowledge graphs, and reinforcement learning.

I enjoy connecting theoretical ideas with practical systems. See my research and projects or read my full CV.

Always happy to connect about AI, machine learning, and research.
